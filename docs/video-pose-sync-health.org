* 1. Why

Four cameras can all remain ONLINE while their timelines drift.

Therefore camera health alone is not enough. Live synchronizer now exposes a separate sync health section.

* 2. Metrics

Tracked on reference-camera attempts:

- reference_frames_total.
- emitted_total.
- miss_total.
- missing_buffer_total.
- tolerance_miss_total.
- stale_reference_total.
- success_ratio.
- last/max skew.
- skew P95/P99.
- current signed offset for every camera.

* 4. Soak defaults

When sync metrics are enabled:

- miss ratio <= 10%.
- P99 skew <= 20 ms.
- emitted synchronized frame sets >= 1.

Offline/manual health snapshots without sync enabled are not affected.

* 5. Interpretation

High missing_buffer_total often means one camera is late or reconnecting.

High tolerance_miss_total with all cameras ONLINE usually means clock offset, transport jitter or decode buffering needs attention.

High stale_reference_total means reference timestamps are not monotonic and should block production acceptance.
